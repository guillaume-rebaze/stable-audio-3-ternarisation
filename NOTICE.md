# Notices and redistribution boundaries

This repository is a personal research/engineering extraction. It is not an
official Stable Audio 3 release and does not grant rights to the upstream model,
dataset, runtime or generated audio beyond the permissions already held by the
user.

The dense Stable Audio 3 teacher is **not** redistributed. The compact artifact
is a derived research package and is labelled `license: other` until the
upstream Stable Audio 3 terms and any applicable dataset/audio terms have been
reviewed for the intended publication channel.

Before redistributing the model, generated WAVs or training evidence, check:

- the current Stable Audio 3 model licence and usage restrictions;
- the MLX/runtime licence and any bundled dependency licences;
- the licences and consent status of datasets, prompts and reference audio;
- the terms of the target Git hosting and Hugging Face repositories.

The repository documents provenance hashes and the exact derived scope to make
that review possible. It does not replace the upstream legal terms.
